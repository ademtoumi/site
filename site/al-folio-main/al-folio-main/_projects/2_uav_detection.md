UAV-oriented object detection and tracking project conducted at the Electronic Systems Research and Development Unit (URD Laboratory), Sidi Bel Abbès.

## Work Performed

- Prepared aerial object-detection datasets including **VisDrone** and military vehicle imagery for YOLO-based training.
- Trained **YOLOv11s** on VisDrone data; evaluated on the validation set, achieving **mAP@0.5 ≈ 0.85**.
- Implemented **HyperDet** as an alternative detection backbone for UAV imagery.
- Applied **DeepSort** and **CSRT** multi-object tracking algorithms.
- Assessed edge-inference performance on **Raspberry Pi 5** with **Intel Movidius NCS 2**.

## Technologies

`Python` `PyTorch` `Ultralytics YOLOv11` `OpenCV` `DeepSort`
