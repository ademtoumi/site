AI &amp; Data Science Engineer trained at the Higher School of Computer Science (ESI-SBA), Algeria. My research focuses on **log-based anomaly detection**, **explainable AI (XAI)**, **computer vision**, and **natural language processing** in distributed systems, with an emphasis on reproducible experimentation, maintainable architecture, and rigorous technical documentation.

My graduate work spans two theses: an engineering thesis implementing and benchmarking eleven ML and DL models for log anomaly detection across HDFS, BGL, and Spirit datasets with SHAP and LIME explainability; and a Master's thesis providing a systematic survey and comparative synthesis of log-based anomaly detection methods.

I combine solid machine learning theory with practical software engineering to design, evaluate, and deploy reliable algorithms and production-ready applications.
