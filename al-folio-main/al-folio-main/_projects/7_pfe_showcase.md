Defense-grade single-page web application presenting the PFE experimental results across **nine animated sections**, built entirely on real experimental data.

## Sections

1. **Hero** — Animated introduction with thesis title and scope.
2. **Overview** — Project context and objectives.
3. **Datasets** — Interactive explorer for HDFS, BGL, and Spirit datasets.
4. **Pipeline** — 6-step interactive pipeline diagram (from raw logs to predictions).
5. **Results** — Filterable benchmark table with bar, radar, and scatter charts.
6. **XAI Gallery** — SHAP and LIME visualizations per model and dataset.
7. **Literature** — Comparison with published state-of-the-art results.
8. **Real-Time Demo** — Architecture diagram of the Kafka–Streamlit pipeline.
9. **Future Work** — Identified limitations and research directions.
